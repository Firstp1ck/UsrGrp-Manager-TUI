Architecture and implementation

Secrets

No password-bearing argv

SecretString and PasswordRecord are non-debug, zeroizing, and stdin-only. Sudo authentication and chpasswd payload transport remain separate protocols.

Identity

Fail closed

Effective UID uses checked Linux APIs. Root is unconditionally immutable; service identities and elevation groups require an explicit injected policy.

Operations

One exact composite plan

Create/password/membership, password/expiry, and bulk actions share one validated preview and one aggregate completion/partial report.

Effects

Rendering is pure

Filesystem/process reads are outside src/ui. Diagnostics and membership edges are bounded and cached before frames render.
